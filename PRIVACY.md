# Privacy Policy for SnapshotAll

SnapshotAll is an open-source, local-first utility. 

- **No Data Collection:** SnapshotAll does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, or telemetry.
- **Local Processing:** All snapshots, images, and data generated by the application are processed and stored exclusively on your local machine.
- **Third Parties:** The application does not communicate with any third-party servers or tracking services, with two exceptions that only happen when you start them and that send no data about you or your files: the first Android (`.apk`) scan downloads the Android runtime (Java runtime from Adoptium, emulator and system image from Google), and the first website scan may download Chromium if no browser is installed. Programs and apps you capture run on your own computer or in the local emulator.
